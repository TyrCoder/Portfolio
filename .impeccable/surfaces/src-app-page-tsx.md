## Scope

The single-page portfolio at `src/app/page.tsx` and its sections. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience and job

Hiring screeners, engineering leads, and OJT/practicum coordinators at IT companies, opening a link from an application and deciding in under a minute whether this student is worth an interview or a practicum slot. Action: reach the work, then the contact line or the GitHub profile. Proof: the artifact they are standing inside, plus the repository behind it.

## Constraints

Owner-pinned and binding: no gradient color, no basic colors, no basic buttons, no basic fonts, no source comments. Projects must paginate. All owner content is placeholder until supplied and must never read as a real credential.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A junior developer presented as a set of drawings that were actually issued, stamped, and built — the page is a drawing sheet, not a document about one. It refuses the category arrangement: greeting headline, short role line, two buttons, grid of equal cards on a near-black ground with one glowing accent.

OWN-WORLD: A plotter sheet on cool bond, not warm cream. Ground `#E8EAE6`, plotter black `#14161A`, graphite `#565B62`, hairline rules `#C2C7C1`, redline vermilion `#E5411E` for every active and primary mark, non-photo blue `#4E86A6` for construction and secondary marks. Flat fills only. Plot space is the dark counterpart, same drawing, lights out. Archivo carries the voice across its width axis — expanded for display, normal for reading; Martian Mono is reserved for measurements, sheet numbers, coordinates, and dates. Components are drawing furniture: sheet border with grid references, a title block locked to the bottom edge, revision tables, dimension lines with ticks, leader lines to annotations, hatch fills. Depth is drawn through line weight and ink density; there are no generic drop shadows.

STORY: The visitor understands within one screen that this is an IT student who can finish things to a professional standard. They believe it because the page itself is finished to that standard, down to the caret, the selection, and the scrollbar. They act by paging through the project sheets and taking the contact line or the repository link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed sheet with a hairline border and lettered/numbered grid references on all four edges. The name is set in expanded Archivo at display scale in the upper-left drawing field, dimension lines running beneath it with the role stated as a measured span. A redline annotation with a leader line sits at the upper right, carrying the availability note. The title block is locked to the bottom edge across the full width: sheet number, scale, date, revision, status, and the primary action as a redline-stamped control sitting in its rightmost cell. Everything commits in one contact; nothing waits for a scroll to be legible.

FORM: The drafting plate, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered grounded list, assigned by seed key `443afe0d`. Raised by named donations: addressable sheet numbers bound to the URL (from Teletext Service), isolate-on-select with the field dimming (from Collider Event Display), depth drawn through line weight rather than shadow (from Akari), explicit issue status per project (from Raku), and a first viewport that commits everything in one contact (from Ebru).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment

Paging the project sheets: the sheet number in the title block advances, the drawing field re-plots the next set with line work resolving from construction weight to final weight, and the URL carries the sheet address so any page is linkable.
